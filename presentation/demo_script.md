# Zecpath AI — Demo Script

## 1. Opening

Good morning everyone.

Today I am presenting Zecpath AI, an AI-powered recruitment and interview intelligence platform.

The system brings multiple candidate evaluation stages together into a structured hiring workflow.

## 2. Problem

Recruiters may need to review many resumes and candidate responses.

Candidate information can also be spread across different evaluation stages.

Zecpath helps organize this information and provides structured evaluation results.

## 3. AI Solution

The system supports:

- Resume parsing
- ATS evaluation
- AI screening
- HR interview evaluation
- Technical evaluation
- Behavioral analysis
- Communication analysis
- Integrity evaluation
- Machine-test evaluation
- Unified scoring
- Candidate ranking
- Final recommendation
- Hiring intelligence

## 4. Architecture

The system starts with candidate and job information.

The information moves through the AI evaluation layer.

The results are then processed through scoring and decision intelligence.

Finally, structured candidate results and recommendations are produced.

## 5. Demo

For the demonstration, I will use the existing demo dataset.

There are three candidate scenarios.

CAND001 represents a strong candidate.
