\# Final Recommendation AI Framework



\## 1. Purpose



The Final Recommendation AI provides a structured decision-making system for automated hiring outcomes.



The system combines candidate performance scores with behavioral and integrity risk factors.



\## 2. Decision Categories



The system supports three final decision categories:



\- Selected

\- Hold / Review

\- Rejected



\## 3. Input Factors



The Final Recommendation AI uses:



\- Overall candidate score

\- Behavioral score

\- Integrity risk score



\## 4. Decision Process



The processing flow is:



Candidate Scores

↓

Risk Evaluation

↓

Rule Evaluation

↓

Recommendation Generation

↓

Confidence Calculation

↓

Risk Factor Identification

↓

Explainable Decision Output



\## 5. Hybrid Decision Logic



The system combines score-based evaluation with rule-based risk conditions.



A strong overall score and acceptable behavioral indicators can support a Selected decision.



Moderate scores may result in Hold / Review.



High integrity risk can result in Rejected regardless of the overall score.



\## 6. Confidence Score



The system calculates a confidence score using:



\- Overall score

\- Behavioral score

\- Integrity risk



The confidence value is represented on a scale of 0 to 100.



\## 7. Risk Factors



The system identifies relevant risk factors including:



\- Behavioral concerns

\- High integrity risk

\- No major risk factors detected



\## 8. Explainability



The final output retains the input scores, recommendation, confidence score, risk factors, and explanation.



This allows reviewers to understand how the final recommendation was generated.



\## 9. Expected Outcome



The Final Recommendation AI provides a structured and explainable method for producing final hiring recommendations.
