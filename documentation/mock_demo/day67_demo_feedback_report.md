## 5. Weak Areas Identified

The following areas should receive additional attention during the final demo:

1. Avoid spending too much time explaining individual source-code modules.
2. Keep the architecture explanation at a high level.
3. Clearly distinguish demonstration dataset values from live production data.
4. Explain candidate scores in simple terms.
5. Avoid claiming future-scope functionality as currently implemented.
6. Keep the transition between slides smooth.
7. Focus on business value rather than only technical implementation.
8. Maintain a controlled demonstration pace.

## 6. Recommended Improvements

### Explanation

Use simple language when explaining technical concepts.

### Architecture

Explain the purpose of each layer instead of describing every file.

### Candidate Results

Explain why candidates receive different results rather than only reading the numbers.

### Business Value

Connect technical capabilities to recruiter benefits.

### Future Scope

Clearly label planned capabilities as future development.

## 7. Stakeholder Q&A Preparation

Important questions to prepare for include:

### What problem does Zecpath AI solve?

Zecpath AI is designed to support recruitment by structuring candidate evaluation across multiple recruitment stages.

### How does the system evaluate candidates?

Candidate information can be processed through stages such as resume/ATS evaluation, screening, HR evaluation, technical evaluation, behavioral and communication analysis, integrity evaluation, scoring, ranking, and recommendation.

### Why use multiple evaluation stages?

Multiple stages provide a broader candidate assessment rather than depending on a single evaluation result.

### Can recruiters understand the AI results?

The system includes structured outputs, candidate ranking, recommendations, and hiring intelligence intended to make results easier for recruiters to review.

### Is the system production-ready?

The project has undergone testing, performance work, monitoring, security/governance work, stabilization, internal review, and feature polishing. Final production deployment would still require appropriate real-world validation and integration.

### Are all planned recruitment features implemented?

No. The presentation distinguishes the demonstrated project functionality from future scope.

### What data is being used for the demonstration?

The demonstration uses a prepared demo dataset containing job descriptions, candidate resumes, candidate responses, and simulated evaluation results.

### How can the system be improved?

Future improvements can include additional integrations, expanded automation, improved analytics, and further AI capabilities.

## 8. Timing Review

Recommended presentation structure:

- Introduction and problem: 1–2 minutes
- AI solution: 1–2 minutes
- Architecture and pipeline: 2–3 minutes
- AI modules: 1–2 minutes
- Candidate demonstration: 4–6 minutes
- Business impact: 1–2 minutes
- Production readiness and future scope: 1–2 minutes
- Q&A: remaining time

Target presentation duration:

Approximately 15–20 minutes, excluding extended stakeholder Q&A.

## 9. Confidence Improvement

Before the final demonstration:

- Practice the opening several times.
- Practice explaining the architecture without reading the slide.
- Practice the candidate demonstration using the same sequence each time.
- Prepare short answers for common technical and business questions.
- Avoid memorizing every sentence.
- Focus on understanding the system flow.
