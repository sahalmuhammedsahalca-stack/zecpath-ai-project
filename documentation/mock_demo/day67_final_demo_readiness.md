15–20 minutes excluding extended Q&A.

## 6. Communication Readiness

Recommended communication approach:

- Speak clearly.
- Use simple explanations.
- Avoid unnecessary technical terminology.
- Explain the purpose before the implementation details.
- Pause between major sections.
- Allow stakeholders to ask questions.
- Do not rush through candidate results.

## 7. Accuracy Rules

During the final demo:

1. Do not claim future-scope features as implemented.
2. Clearly identify simulated demonstration results.
3. Explain that AI outputs support recruiter decision-making.
4. Avoid presenting demo data as real production candidate data.
5. Explain scores in the context of their evaluation stage.
