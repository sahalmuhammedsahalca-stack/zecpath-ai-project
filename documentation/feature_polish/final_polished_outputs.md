# Day 65 – Final Polished Outputs

## Candidate Evaluation Output

The polished candidate output contains:

- Candidate ID
- Overall score
- Recommendation
- Strengths
- Concerns

Example:

Candidate: CAND001
Overall Score: 90.25/100
Recommendation: Highly Recommended

---

## Scoring Output

Scores are normalized to two decimal places.

Example:

ATS: 92.00
Screening: 90.00
HR: 88.00
Technical: 91.00

Overall: 90.25/100

---

## Error Output

Errors are returned in a consistent structure.

Example:

success: False
error_type: missing_candidate
message: Candidate information is missing.

---

## Recruiter Ranking

Candidates can be ranked by overall evaluation score.

The highest scoring candidate appears first.

---

## Final Result

The enhanced outputs provide clearer, more consistent and recruiter-friendly evaluation information.
