# Innovation Proposal

## Objective

Expand Zecpath AI from an interview evaluation platform into an intelligent
candidate development and interview analytics platform.

## Proposed Innovations

### 1. AI Video Analysis

Analyse future video interviews to generate structured visual interview
signals.

### 2. Emotion Detection

Provide optional emotional-state signals with confidence and uncertainty
information.

### 3. Real-Time Feedback

Generate structured feedback during or immediately after interviews.

### 4. AI Coaching

Provide personalised recommendations to help candidates improve.

### 5. Candidate Improvement Suggestions

Automatically identify weak areas from interview performance.

### 6. Interview Analytics Dashboard

Provide recruiters and authorised users with structured performance
analytics.

## Expected Benefits

- Better candidate feedback
- Better interview analysis
- More useful candidate development
- Improved recruiter insights
- Future scalability
