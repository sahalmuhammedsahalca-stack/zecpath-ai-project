\# Hiring Intelligence Report Generation Logic



\## 1. Purpose



The Report Generation Logic defines how candidate evaluation information is converted into a structured hiring intelligence report.



\## 2. Input Information



The report may use:



\- ATS score

\- Screening score

\- HR interview score

\- Technical score

\- Behavioral score

\- Integrity risk score

\- Hiring fit percentage

\- Final recommendation

\- Confidence score



\## 3. Score Summary



The system should organize the available evaluation scores into a single summary.



\## 4. Strength Detection



Candidate strengths should be identified from strong evaluation results.



High-performing evaluation areas may be included as candidate strengths.



\## 5. Weakness Detection



Low-performing evaluation areas may be identified as weaknesses.



The system should clearly distinguish weaknesses from risk indicators.



\## 6. Risk Detection



The report should include relevant risk factors from the candidate evaluation.



High integrity risk and behavioral concerns should be highlighted when present.



\## 7. Recommendation



The final recommendation should be taken from the Final Recommendation AI.



The report should not independently replace the final recommendation.



\## 8. Explanation



The report should provide a clear explanation of the candidate's evaluation.



\## 9. Report Structure



The generated report should contain:



1\. Candidate Information

2\. Overall Evaluation

3\. Score Summary

4\. Strengths

5\. Weaknesses

6\. Risk Indicators

7\. Final Recommendation

8\. Confidence

9\. Recruiter Summary



\## 10. Expected Outcome



The Report Generation Logic provides a consistent method for transforming candidate evaluation results into a recruiter-friendly hiring intelligence report.
