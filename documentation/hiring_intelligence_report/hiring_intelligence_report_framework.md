\# Hiring Intelligence Report Framework



\## 1. Purpose



The Hiring Intelligence Report provides recruiters with a complete AI-generated evaluation of a candidate.



The report combines evaluation results from multiple stages of the recruitment process into one structured candidate profile.



\## 2. Evaluation Sources



The report combines:



\- ATS evaluation

\- Screening results

\- HR interview insights

\- Technical interview performance

\- Behavioral analysis

\- Integrity and risk indicators

\- Final recommendation



\## 3. Candidate Profile



The report should provide a consolidated view of the candidate including:



\- Candidate identification

\- Job role

\- Overall evaluation scores

\- Hiring fit

\- Interview performance

\- Technical performance

\- Behavioral observations

\- Risk indicators



\## 4. Strengths



The system should identify important candidate strengths from available evaluation results.



Examples include:



\- Strong technical skills

\- Strong communication

\- Good problem-solving ability

\- Strong behavioral performance

\- High hiring fit



\## 5. Weaknesses



The system should identify areas where the candidate may require improvement.



Examples include:



\- Technical skill gaps

\- Communication concerns

\- Behavioral concerns

\- Low evaluation scores

\- Performance inconsistencies



\## 6. Risk Indicators



The report should identify relevant risk indicators.



Possible indicators include:



\- High integrity risk

\- Behavioral concerns

\- Low technical performance

\- Low screening performance

\- Inconsistent evaluation results



\## 7. Final Recommendation



The report should include the final hiring recommendation generated by the Final Recommendation AI.



Possible outcomes:



\- Selected

\- Hold / Review

\- Rejected



\## 8. Recruiter-Friendly Format



The report should be organized into clear sections so recruiters can quickly understand the candidate's overall profile.



\## 9. Explainability



The report should preserve the underlying scores, strengths, weaknesses, risks, and recommendation explanation.



\## 10. Expected Outcome



The Hiring Intelligence Report provides recruiters with a complete and structured AI-generated candidate evaluation report.
